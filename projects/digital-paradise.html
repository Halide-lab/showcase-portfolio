BRANDING

A complete brand identity and digital experience design for a DJ that represents energy, freedom and a connection between digital and real life.

The visual identity was inspired by electronic music culture, nightlife aesthetics and modern digital branding. Bright orange tones, bold typography and high-contrast layouts were used to create an energetic and recognizable visual style.

Role Brand Identity, Web Design, Social Media

Tools Figma, Canva, HTML, CSS, JS

Duration 6 weeks
