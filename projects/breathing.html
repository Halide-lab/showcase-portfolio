Breathing

Exercise App

MOBILE APP DESIGN

A minimal breathing exercise app designed to help users slow down, relax and focus on their breathing.

The app was designed to encourage mindfulness and relaxation through minimal interactions, calming visuals and guided breathing sessions. The main goal was to help users slow down and focus on their breathing in a more accessible and user-friendly way.

Role UI/UX Design, Prototyping, Coding

Tools Figma, HTML, CSS, JS

Duration 3 weeks
